The authors select a small set of examples to label by minimizing Sinkhorn divergence in a pretrained representation space. The criterion gives dimension-free sample complexity and permits gradient-based search followed by local swaps. On image and medical benchmarks, the method improves on competing heuristics when labels are scarce.
