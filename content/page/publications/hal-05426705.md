The authors extend Frank-Wolfe methods with two corrective steps for convex quadratic objectives and give conditions for finite-time convergence. The corrections also accelerate the quadratic subproblems in split conditional gradient and second-order conditional gradient sliding. Experiments demonstrate substantial reductions in computation time.
