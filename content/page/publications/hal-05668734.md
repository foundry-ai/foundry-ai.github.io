Dri-MED addresses contextual bandits with user-specific preferences and changing context distributions. It adapts exploration to time-varying noise and accounts for a control policy’s reward, with guarantees on regret and violations of the baseline constraint. The authors compare it experimentally with approaches that ignore preference and context drift.
