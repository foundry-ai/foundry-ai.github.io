Adaptive quasimetric mapping estimates travel times from offline trajectories and uses them to build a sparse navigation graph. During deployment it removes edges whose observed traversal time exceeds the estimated budget, allowing the agent to replan as obstacles change. The method achieves competitive results on navigation tasks while using up to 100 times fewer keypoints than prior approaches.
