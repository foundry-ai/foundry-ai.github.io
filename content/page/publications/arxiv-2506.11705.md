The authors analyze a heavy-ball system with Hessian-driven damping for smooth non-convex objectives satisfying a Łojasiewicz condition. They derive worst-case convergence rates tied to the damping coefficients and the Łojasiewicz exponent, and study stability under gradient errors and avoidance of strict saddles.
