The authors combine a rank-based estimate of the Cramér-von Mises sensitivity index with differentiable soft ranking to penalize dependence between predictions and a sensitive attribute. The method reduces the cost per batch to near-linear time and achieves competitive fairness and utility on tabular and image datasets.
