<p>We study the finite-sample convergence of Q-learning algorithms for the average-reward formulation of Reinforcement Learning (RL) with options. While options allow temporarily extended and hierarchical actions, the average-reward formulation incorporates continual tasks beyond discounted and episodic RL. We establish the first \(\tilde{O}(1/\text{iterations})\) convergence guarantees, in the mean-square sense, for average-reward option Q-learning algorithms in both the inter- and intra-option settings. First, we prove that both inter- and intra-option Bellman operators satisfy multi-step span contractions under unichain assumptions. Then, we propose synchronous estimators of these multi-step option Bellman operators leading to two variants of option Q-learning. Finally, we extend the stochastic approximation framework to prove convergence rates of these two algorithms under the minimal assumptions used in the literature.</p>
