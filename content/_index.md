FOUNDRY is a [PEPR IA](https://www.pepr-ia.fr/en/pepr/) research project funded under the [France 2030](https://www.info.gouv.fr/grand-dossier/france-2030) programme by the [French National Research Agency (ANR)](https://anr.fr/en/). Its overarching objective is to develop the mathematical foundations and methods needed for machine learning systems to remain reliable under incomplete or corrupted data, distribution shifts, changing conditions, or in the presence of competing agents or conflicting goals.

The consortium brings together [six research teams](page/team/) in Grenoble ([GHOST](https://ghost-team.gitlabpages.inria.fr/)), Lyon ([ENS Lyon](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/)), Paris ([FAIRPLAY](https://team.inria.fr/fairplay/), [LTCI](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/fr/recherche/labos/traitement-information-ltci) and [MILES](https://miles.lamsade.fr/)), and Lille ([SCOOL](https://team.inria.fr/scool/)). The project started in July 2023 and will last until 31 December 2028
